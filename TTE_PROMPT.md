# Toot-Toot Engineering Prompt

In the big picture, we are making a movie built from svg animations about One Drop, a drop of rain that awakens, as the first person narrative viewpoint, falling from the sky, like the point of view of a sky diver, facing the ground, falling towards earth with dozens of other sky divers around also falling, only the sky divers are other rain drops, near and far.  As a group, they fall, first through a blue field towards a big white cloud that gets bigger and bigger as we get closer and closer, then using transparent fog, we fall through the cloud and the view open up and we are directly above a mountain lake, first small like a puddle, then bigger and bigger until we fall completely into the blueness.

First create an svg animation of One Drop, as one_drop_portrait.svg. Use just an opaque blue outline oval, a small white reflective bubble oval, and transparent blue infill. Use the suggestive appearance and behavior of a cute anime character to bring these simple lines and colors to life as One Drop, a drop of water we can identify with.

Next build the scene, raining.svg as a long, continuous shot, facing the ground, as we and dozens of other rain drops, around, fall from an opening fade-in from black, though blue sky towards clouds below us, then into a big cloud, falling through white fog, then emerging to see a mountain lake that we fall into. As we approach, we can see many rain drops splashing into the lake, more and more until we ourselves, sink into the blue.
