# STORYTELLER (cycle-01)

## Narrative focus
A horn-grill shape that feels soft-edged and functional: a smooth, confident perimeter that suggests airflow without looking industrial. The form should read as hand-drawn but purposeful, translating a sketch into a clean, printable artifact.

## Narrative beats
- Gentle curvature to keep the object friendly and approachable.
- Consistent perimeter thickness to imply durability.
- A grounded, stable base to suggest reliable mounting or placement.
