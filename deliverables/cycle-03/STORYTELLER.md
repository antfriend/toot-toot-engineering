# STORYTELLER (cycle-03)

## Narrative lens
The viewer is One Drop: awake, curious, and in motion. The film grammar is first-person descent, where scale change tells emotion.

## Story beat map
1. **Awakening**: darkness opens to blue; movement starts gentle.
2. **Commitment to fall**: nearby droplets establish community and velocity.
3. **Threshold**: the cloud grows from distant mass to surrounding atmosphere.
4. **Disorientation**: fog wraps the frame and reduces certainty.
5. **Revelation**: fog thins and the mountain lake appears, first tiny then immense.
6. **Arrival**: many impacts on water signal inevitability and belonging.
7. **Merge**: camera/viewpoint sinks into the blue body of the lake.

## Character direction for One Drop portrait
- Keep anatomy minimal and iconic.
- Convey personality through motion timing: soft bob, slight tilt, and reflective highlight drift.
- Keep the palette emotionally readable: clear blue identity, soft translucent core, white spark of awareness.

## Scene direction for raining.svg
- Preserve one unbroken shot.
- Prioritize depth progression over object detail.
- Use raindrop density and scale changes to signal acceleration.
- Use cloud/fog as a visual tunnel between sky and lake.

## Emotional target
Curiosity -> wonder -> surrender.
