# STORYTELLER (cycle-04)

## Narrative focus
The monitor is the “control tower” for TTN. It listens quietly, then summarizes who is speaking and how often. When needed, it can also export machine-readable logs for later analysis.

## UX narrative beats
- **Lightweight by default:** no dependencies, no curses.
- **Readable flow:** each message line mirrors the node logs.
- **Summary heartbeat:** periodic rollups of node activity.
